# Research Sources and Validation Notes

Research cutoff: 2 August 2026  
Jurisdiction: India, with Uttar Pradesh-specific sources where available

## How to read this source register

The main documents cite sources as `[S1]`, `[S2]`, and so on. Each entry below states what the source supports and what it does not establish. Primary government or professional-regulator sources are used for legal and compliance claims. Commercial sources are used only as market benchmarks.

## S1. Ministry of Labour and Employment: Compliance Handbook under the Four Labour Codes

Link: [Compliance Handbook for Employers Under the Four Labour Codes](https://www.labour.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/83978455025732b99b0165def80ab171.pdf)

What it supports:

- The four labor codes became effective from 21 November 2025.
- Appointment letters, working-hour, overtime, wage-record, deduction, and safety obligations.
- Contract-labor provisions in the OSH Code apply at the stated 50-worker threshold, while the practical employee-versus-independent-business analysis still depends on the real relationship.
- EPF coverage at 20 or more employees and ESI coverage at 10 or more persons, subject to the Code and implementation conditions described in the handbook.
- Aggregator contributions for gig and platform-worker schemes are described as 1%-2% of annual turnover, capped at 5% of worker payouts.
- Employee-compensation and commute-related accident provisions.

Limit:

The handbook is a national compliance summary. It does not replace the UP rules, the current district or scheduled-employment wage notification, or advice on the exact classification of Comfort Curators workers.

## S2. Ministry of Labour and Employment: March 2026 Labour Code FAQs

Link: [Additional FAQs on Labour Codes, 16 March 2026](https://www.labour.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/a4ccf4c6d97c4f1f36a6d83f8c64213d.pdf)

What it supports:

- Minimum wages and agreed wages are not the same, and an employer cannot pay below the applicable minimum.
- Overtime protection and the twice-normal-rate rule described in the FAQ.
- The Code wage definition applies from 21 November 2025.
- Fixed-term employment refers to employees directly engaged by the employer.
- ESI’s referenced ₹21,000 monthly wage ceiling remained applicable pending finalization described by the FAQ.
- Wage deductions are constrained, and the national Code on Wages FAQ separately identifies a 50% cap.

Limit:

The FAQ is informational and expressly does not resolve a fact-specific worker-classification or payroll dispute.

## S3. Child and Adolescent Labour law

Links:

- [Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act](https://www.labour.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/06/2e8a5a3a8a785a9a96569af94290aee7.pdf)
- [Ministry summary of enforcement and age rules](https://www.labour.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/06/784241214c77ef00b461c60f097c824c.pdf)

What it supports:

- Complete prohibition on employment of children below 14, subject to the Act’s limited exceptions.
- Prohibition on adolescents aged 14-18 in hazardous occupations and processes.

Limit:

The law does not certify that a particular Comfort Curators task is safe or appropriate for a 16-17-year-old. The MVP’s 18+ rule is a stricter operational and privacy choice based on road travel, home entry, lone work, chemicals, and late-service risk.

## S4. Automotive Research Association of India: low-speed electric bikes

Links:

- [ARAI vehicle type-approval scope](https://www.araiindia.com/certification/certification/vehicle)
- [ARAI frequently asked questions](https://www.araiindia.com/faqs)

What it supports:

- ARAI distinguishes low-speed, low-power e-bikes from electric two-wheelers above the 250 W and 25 km/h thresholds that require the broader CMVR type-approval route.
- Higher-powered or faster electric two-wheelers require approval and CMVR compliance.

Limit:

This does not prove a supplier’s bike complies. Comfort Curators must verify the finished product, design speed, controller, battery, charger, applicable component standards, and documents. Import classification and product obligations require model-specific review.

## S5. Uttar Pradesh tourism and homestay framework

Links:

- [UP Bed & Breakfast and Homestay Policy 2025 coverage, Invest UP](https://invest.up.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/6-Uttar_020925.pdf)
- [UP approval and fee coverage for the 2025 policy, Invest UP](https://invest.up.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/11-Nod_040625.pdf)
- [Uttar Pradesh Tourism Policy 2022](https://msme1connect.up.gov.in/pdf/Policies/Tourism_Policy_2022.pdf)

What it supports:

- UP has a formal B&B and homestay framework.
- The 2025 policy coverage describes registration of one to six rooms with up to twelve beds and distinguishes rural and urban registration treatment.
- The 2022 tourism policy confirms that B&B and homestay operations are a recognized tourism-unit category and that registered units receive policy treatment.

Limit:

The accessible 2025 government links are policy coverage documents rather than a complete district operating manual. Exact NOC, renewal, municipal, society, police, fire, guest-record, tax, and property-use requirements must be confirmed for each district and building.

## S6. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, Rules, and commencement

Links:

- [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)
- [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)
- [November 2025 phased commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf)

What it supports:

- The Act defines a child as an individual under 18.
- Notice, consent, data-minimization, security, breach, grievance, access, correction, erasure, processor-contract, and child-data duties.
- The 13 November 2025 notification brings provisions into force on different dates, including an eighteen-month delay for most substantive processing sections listed in the notification.
- The Rules specify reasonable safeguards and child or guardian consent mechanisms when the relevant provisions commence.

Limit:

This package does not claim every substantive duty was already in force on 2 August 2026. It recommends building to the scheduled standard before full commencement.

## S7. UIDAI: Aadhaar authentication and offline verification

Links:

- [Aadhaar Authentication and Offline Verification Regulations](https://uidai.gov.in/images/The_Aadhaar_Authentication_and_Offline_Verifications_Regulations_2021.pdf)
- [UIDAI offline verification playbook](https://uidai.gov.in/images/OVSE_Playbook.pdf)

What it supports:

- Aadhaar use for private verification is voluntary and must have a viable alternative in the circumstances described.
- Offline verification requires purpose notice, consent, digital-signature validation, security, and restricted use.
- Storage must be minimized and protected; consent withdrawal can trigger deletion requirements.
- Core biometric data must not be retained.

Limit:

Comfort Curators must determine whether it will become an Offline Verification Seeking Entity or use an authorized verification vendor. A photocopy alone is not a safe technical verification design.

## S8. Food Safety and Standards Authority of India: April 2026 business eligibility

Link: [FoSCoS Kind of Business Eligibility, updated 1 April 2026](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/assets/docs/Revised_31thMarch2026KindofBusinessEligibility.pdf)

What it supports:

- Food manufacturing, storage, transport, distribution, retail, and e-commerce are separately recognized activities.
- The 2026 schedule sets registration or licence categories and thresholds by kind of business.
- E-commerce food activity is identified as requiring central licensing in the schedule.

Limit:

The correct licence depends on the exact entity, premise, products, turnover, and activities. The package therefore recommends no prepared food in the MVP and requires a specific FSSAI check before enabling food SKUs.

## S12. Institute of Chartered Accountants of India: statutory audit authority

Link: [ICAI notes on Audit and Auditors under the Companies Act](https://live.icai.org/bos/vcc-3rd-batch/pdf/Audit_and_Auditors_Notes.pdf)

What it supports:

- Under the Companies Act framework discussed, only eligible Chartered Accountants or qualifying CA firms may act as statutory company auditors and sign the audit report.

Limit:

This does not prevent trained staff from doing bookkeeping, internal control support, reconciliation, or audit preparation. It prevents Comfort Curators from presenting unqualified workers or an LLM as a statutory auditor.

## S13. Indian Computer Emergency Response Team: cyber incident and log directions

Links:

- [CERT-In Directions under section 70B](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf)
- [CERT-In cyber-security directions FAQ](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/FAQs_on_CyberSecurityDirections_May2022.pdf)

What it supports:

- Covered entities must report specified cyber incidents within six hours of noticing them or being informed.
- Applicable ICT logs must be enabled and retained securely for a rolling 180 days within Indian jurisdiction.
- A point of contact and incident process are required in covered circumstances.

Limit:

The exact logs and reportability depend on the event and entity. A security professional should convert the directions into the final incident runbook.

## S14. Current Indian e-bike price benchmarks

Links:

- [Hero Lectro e-bike collection](https://herolectro.com/collections/all-e-bikes)
- [Hero Lectro Y5+](https://herolectro.com/pages/y5-plus)
- [EMotorad T-Rex Air](https://www.emotorad.com/bikes/t-rex-air)

What it supports:

- Current listed examples of supported Indian 250 W e-bikes are roughly in the ₹29,000-₹37,000 range for practical models, with higher-priced options also available.
- Listed products vary materially in battery capacity, range claims, payload, warranty, and service.

Limit:

Retail list price is not a fleet quote or lifecycle cost. Range is vendor-stated and may fall under cargo, heat, hills, traffic, battery aging, and throttle use. Comfort Curators needs a sample trial and commercial quote.

## S17. Uttar Pradesh Labour Department: current wage and establishment source

Links:

- [UP Labour Commissioner Organisation](https://uplabour.gov.in/lc/index)
- [UP Labour inspections and checklists](https://uplabour.gov.in/Pages/IPC)

What it supports:

- The official UP portal publishes current minimum-wage, registration, circular, form, and inspection materials.
- The portal showed revised wage material effective from 1 April 2026 during this research.

Limit and deliberate omission:

Search results exposed conflicting secondary summaries and multiple wage schedules or zones. The main package therefore does not quote a supposed universal UP wage. Before issuing an offer, payroll must retrieve the current official notification for the exact district, zone, skill, and scheduled employment and preserve it with the wage decision.
